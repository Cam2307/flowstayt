What you get

The free extension lets you keep a blocklist, block the site open in the current tab, run 25- and 50-minute focus sessions, and see daily blocked-visit stats. Pro adds a password lock, schedules, allowlist-only mode, and sync across devices. The extension can change as it is updated. Chrome can also limit what an extension is allowed to do, including that you can still turn the extension off from Chrome’s own extensions page.
