Privacy policy

Effective 2 November 2026.

Focus Blocker is a Chrome extension made by Cameron Reece Allcock. This page says what it stores, and what it does not.

Settings and stats stay on the device

The extension stores your settings and stats locally in the browser, using chrome.storage.local. That includes the blocklist, focus sessions, schedules, allowlist, daily blocked-visit counts, and, if you set a password on settings, a hash of that password. The password itself is not stored.

For Pro, the blocklist, schedule, and allowlist are also stored in chrome.storage.sync, which Chrome ties to your Google account so those settings can follow you to other devices where you are signed in to Chrome. Stats and the password stay on the device. They are not synced.

No browsing history, and no sale of data

Focus Blocker collects no browsing history. It does not send the sites you visit, the pages you open, or your blocked-visit counts to Cameron Reece Allcock or to anyone else. No browsing data leaves the device. We sell no data.

When you open a site that is on your blocklist, the extension redirects that visit to its own blocked page. That decision happens on your device. We do not receive a copy of it.

Payments and licence keys

Pro is paid for through Lemon Squeezy. Payments and licence keys go through Lemon Squeezy, which is the merchant of record. Lemon Squeezy handles checkout and tax. Focus Blocker does not receive your card number. What we need in order to recognise a paid licence is limited to details such as a licence key and the email used at checkout, provided by Lemon Squeezy.
