How it works

Three clicks to a quieter browser.

  1. 1

    Add your distractions

    Type a site or hit Block current site on the tab that keeps pulling you in.

  2. 2

    Start a focus session

    Pick 25 or 50 minutes. Blocked sites redirect to a calm reminder page until it ends.

  3. 3

    See your progress

    The popup counts today's blocked visits; settings show the last 7 days.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Does any browsing data leave my device?

No. Focus Blocker collects no browsing history and sells no data. The blocklist, settings and stats stay in your browser.

Who handles payment?

Lemon Squeezy is the merchant of record. It runs checkout, tax and licence keys. Focus Blocker never sees your card number.

How do I unlock Pro after buying?

Lemon Squeezy emails you a licence key. Open Focus Blocker's settings, paste the key under Focus Blocker Pro and click Activate.

Can I cancel Pro?

Yes, any time. You keep Pro until the end of the month or year you already paid for, and you won't be charged again.

What does sync copy?

For Pro, the blocklist, schedule and allowlist sync through Chrome sync on your Google account. Daily stats and the settings password stay on each device.
