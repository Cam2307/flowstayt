Focus Blocker

Block distracting websites. No paywall on the basics.

A Chrome extension for the sites you choose to block. The free version is a working blocker: a blocklist, a button for the tab you are on, a 25- or 50-minute focus session, and a count of blocked visits today.

Features

The free extension is the product. Pro adds the controls other blockers charge for before the basics work.

Free

  • Blocklist. Add a site from the popup or from settings. Visits to it are redirected to the blocked page.
  • Block-current-site button. Blocks the tab you are on, without typing the address.
  • 25/50-minute focus sessions. Start a session from the popup. Blocking stays on until it ends.
  • Daily blocked-visit stats. Today’s count is in the popup. Settings shows the last 7 days.

Pro PRO

  • Password lock. Settings ask for a password before they can be changed. The popup can add sites, not remove them.
  • Schedules. Block only on the days and hours you pick, including ranges that run overnight.
  • Allowlist-only mode. Block every site except the ones you allow.
  • Sync across devices. Blocklist, schedule, and allowlist sync with Chrome on your Google account. Stats and the password stay on the device.

FAQ

Does any browsing data leave the device?

No. No browsing data leaves the device. Focus Blocker collects no browsing history and sells no data. The blocklist, settings, and stats stay in the browser. Blocking is decided on your computer.

Who handles payment?

Payments are handled by Lemon Squeezy as merchant of record. Lemon Squeezy runs checkout, tax, and licence keys. Focus Blocker does not take your card number.

Can I cancel Pro?

Yes. Cancel any time. You keep Pro until the end of the month or year you have already paid for, and the next charge does not happen.

What does sync actually copy?

For Pro, the blocklist, schedule, and allowlist are stored in Chrome sync, tied to your Google account. Daily stats and the settings password stay on that device.
