ScooprDoopr privacy policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026 · Developer: Cameron Reece Allcock

Summary

ScooprDoopr has no servers that receive your data. We do not collect, store, sell or share your browsing data, page content, extracted tables or API keys.

What happens when you press Extract

  • Only after you click, ScooprDoopr reads the text of the current tab.
  • That text, plus your column descriptions, is sent directly from your browser to the AI provider you chose (OpenAI, Anthropic, Google or xAI) using your own API key. That provider's privacy policy and data-use terms apply to the request.
  • The results stay in your browser until you export them.

Third-party AI providers

When you click Extract, the page text is sent directly from your browser to the AI provider you selected (OpenAI, Anthropic, Google Gemini or xAI) using your own API key and account. ScooprDoopr never receives that text. The provider's handling and retention of the data is governed by that provider's own terms and privacy policy, which you should review. Avoid extracting pages that contain sensitive or personal information.

  • Google privacy policy (Google Gemini)
  • xAI privacy policy

Stored on your device

Your settings, API keys, saved column templates and (Pro) extraction history are kept in chrome.storage.local on your computer. Uninstalling ScooprDoopr deletes them. You can clear history at any time in Settings.

Licensing

If you buy Pro, payments go through Lemon Squeezy or Polar. Your licence key is checked with that provider when you activate it and about once a day. Only the licence key is sent for verification. Lemon Squeezy also receives an instance id. Polar also receives the public organization id.

Permissions

  • activeTab, scripting: read the page you're on, only when you click.
  • storage: save your settings on your device.
  • alarms: daily licence check.
  • Host access is limited to the four AI provider APIs plus the Lemon Squeezy and Polar licence APIs.
